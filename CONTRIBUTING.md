# How to review or contribute

This is an **open, unproved, AI-assisted research notebook** on aspects of Bałaban's constructive Yang–Mills program. The fastest useful contributions identify a *specific* mathematical or bibliographic issue rather than offering an unqualified proof-completion claim.

## Before opening an issue or pull request

1. Read the [scope and exact open interfaces](TARGET_SCOPE.md), [research-status ledger](RESEARCH_STATUS.md), and [R103 source receipt](ym/source_receipts/YM_R103_CMP99_3_MARKED_DOMAIN_RECEIPT_20261014.md).
2. Name the **native pass, theorem/equation, source page, and repository commit** you are checking.
3. Provide the **full hypotheses**, including group, norms, scales, field domains, quantifiers, and uniformity constants if known. Say explicitly when an original page image or precise notation has **not** been checked.
4. Classify your finding as a **source correction, verified abstract lemma, conditional interface, obstruction/counterexample, or candidate native proof**. Do not mark a source locator or analogy as theorem closure.
5. Explain how your conclusion affects one of the live YM-R103/R104 gates and provide a minimal independent way to audit it.

A minimal report can use:

~~~text
Location: file + section/line + commit
Claim being checked:
Source: original page/equation or stable manuscript version
Exact hypotheses:
Independent calculation / counterexample / correction:
Native obligation affected:
Remaining assumptions:
~~~

## Research-history policy

- Keep completed YM-R1–R103 passes and canonical checkpoints append-only where practicable; use [dated errata](REVIEW_ERRATA_20261026.md) rather than silently rewriting historical claims.
- Do not rename or consume **YM-R104** without conducting the native research audit and updating its evidence ledger.
- Keep speculative comparison **YM-EXT-01** distinct from native theorem proof.
- Third-party manuscripts and original articles should be linked and attributed, not bulk copied. Supply only material you have rights to contribute.
- Declare when text, proof searches, computations, or mathematical statements were AI-assisted, and what was independently checked. Never imply an AI output is formally verified without the actual artifacts and theorem statement.

## Licensing and rights

There is **no repository-wide reuse license confirmed at this time**. Please read [RIGHTS_AND_ATTRIBUTION.md](RIGHTS_AND_ATTRIBUTION.md) before copying, modifying, redistributing, or contributing material. This is a rights-policy hold for the repository owner, **not** a claim that GitHub public visibility grants open-source permission.

Submitting a contribution does not resolve rights in source texts or third-party content. Where contributor permissions are uncertain, begin with an issue or a short citation-based description rather than uploading copied material.

## Communication

Focus review requests on a named proof obligation. External academic correspondence is not automatically authorized by opening a contribution; no affiliated researcher or institution is implied.
