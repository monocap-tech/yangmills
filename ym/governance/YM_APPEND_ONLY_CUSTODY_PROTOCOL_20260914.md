# Yang–Mills append-only custody protocol

Date opened: 2026-09-14
Repository: `monocap-tech/yangmills`

## 1. Authority

Repository custody overrides conversational recollection. Later passes may revise earlier interpretations only explicitly and appenditively; historical state is not silently rewritten.

## 2. Claim discipline

Every durable mathematical statement should be typed as one of:

- **THEOREM / LITERATURE FACT** — source-attested and stated no more strongly than the source.
- **DERIVATION** — follows from recorded hypotheses and prior results.
- **WORKING HYPOTHESIS** — structural interpretation or proposed route.
- **NEGATIVE RESULT** — route, implication, or identification ruled out or shown insufficient.
- **OPEN OBLIGATION** — unresolved theorem deficit, compatibility condition, nonvanishing problem, construction problem, or audit item.

No analogy among lattice, RG, stochastic-quantization, or reconstruction programs is promoted to a theorem merely because their interfaces look compatible.

## 3. Pass custody

Pass numbers are monotone. Each pass records:

1. target question;
2. prior state;
3. literature interfaces;
4. source-attested facts;
5. reconstruction / derivation;
6. negative results;
7. limitations;
8. bounded determination;
9. next frontier.

## 4. Opening scope

The initial laboratory is not a claimed proof of the Yang–Mills existence and mass-gap problem. It begins by typing the relation between lattice mass-gap results, continuum-limit construction, renormalization-group transport, and Osterwalder–Schrader/Wightman spectral reconstruction.

The opening question is:

> What quantitative carrier can survive the continuum limit while the dimensionless lattice mass tends to zero and a nonzero physical mass scale remains?

## 5. Safety boundary

As of the opening checkpoint, the project does **not** claim:

- construction of four-dimensional pure quantum Yang–Mills;
- a proof of a positive continuum mass gap;
- that any existing strong-coupling lattice theorem continues to the asymptotically free continuum limit;
- that Balaban-type RG, strong-coupling functional inequalities, stochastic quantization, and OS reconstruction already share a proved common transport invariant;
- that an RG-invariant scale such as `Lambda_YM` has been rigorously identified with the required physical spectral gap in the needed generality.

The purpose of custody is to prevent exactly these overpromotions.
