## What R105 *actually* deduces independently

1. Elementary algebra: \(\eta_{N+1,j+1}=\eta_{N,j}\).
2. Elementary logical nonimplication: same spacing and separately small plaquette curvatures do not imply equal prescribed \(V\) or paired \(\Omega\) sequences. Near-identity gauge data with different plaquette holonomies give a diagnostic counterexample.
3. Elementary measure distinction: pairing points or deterministic variational minimizers cannot imply pushforward equality or smallness of differences of the normalized cutoff measures. This needs a distinct measure/RG identity or observable comparison, with large-field complement and normalization accounted for.

**No assertion that these counterexamples are themselves genuine Bałaban two-cutoff trajectories.** We do not claim a mathematical no-go theorem for defining a lawful pairing, nor do we assert that the published Bałaban source lacks a result without original-image checks.
