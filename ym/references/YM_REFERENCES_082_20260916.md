## [82] Sourav Chatterjee — The leading term of the Yang–Mills free energy

- Full citation: S. Chatterjee, “The leading term of the Yang–Mills free energy,” *Journal of Functional Analysis* **271**(10), 2944–3005 (2016).
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jfa.2016.04.032.
- Source used: ScienceDirect publisher landing/text and arXiv:1602.01222 metadata.
- Role in traversal: independent retrospective clarification of what Bałaban-style ultraviolet stability does and does not mean. Chatterjee describes ultraviolet stability of the effective action as compactness/stability with respect to the ultraviolet cutoff, while separately noting that rigorous continuum construction of three- and four-dimensional non-Abelian Yang–Mills remains unresolved. This supports the R62 distinction between one-trajectory effective-action stability and cross-cutoff convergence of normalized states/observables.
- Status: PRIMARY peer-reviewed paper / author manuscript metadata.
