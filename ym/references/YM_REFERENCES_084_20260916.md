## [84] Bauerschmidt–Brydges–Slade — Structural stability near a non-hyperbolic fixed point

- Full citation: R. Bauerschmidt, D. C. Brydges, G. Slade, “Structural stability of a dynamical system near a non-hyperbolic fixed point,” *Annales Henri Poincaré* **16** (2015), 1033–1065.
- DOI: 10.1007/s00023-014-0338-0.
- arXiv: 1211.2477.
- Source used: https://arxiv.org/abs/1211.2477
- Role in traversal: theorem-species control for YM-R63. The authors prove structural stability for a class of nonautonomous discrete RG systems near a nonhyperbolic fixed point by solving a mixed initial/final boundary problem in weighted sequence spaces. Their Theorem 1.4 is explicitly described as an analogue of a stable-manifold theorem and gives existence, uniqueness, regularity, cutoff-time-uniform estimates, and continuous dependence for the tuned flow.
- Important jurisdiction note: the theorem is developed for RG systems arising from 4d weakly self-avoiding walk and n-component |phi|^4 models. It is not a theorem about non-Abelian Yang–Mills and cannot be imported without verification of its hypotheses in the Balaban complete-density chart.
- Status: PRIMARY peer-reviewed theorem/control example.
