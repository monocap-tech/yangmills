## [69] Jonathan Dimock — The Renormalization Group According to Balaban, I. Small Fields

- Full citation: J. Dimock, “The Renormalization Group According to Balaban, I. Small Fields,” *Reviews in Mathematical Physics* **25**(7), 1330010 (2013).
- DOI: 10.1142/S0129055X13300100
- Sources used:
  - https://arxiv.org/abs/1108.1335
  - https://doi.org/10.1142/S0129055X13300100
- Role in traversal: YM-R22 methodological control for Bałaban-style analytic domains, Cauchy estimates, random-walk localization, and polymer/cluster-exponentiation structure. The model treated in the paper is scalar phi^4_3, so its constants and model-specific norms are not imported into 4d Yang–Mills.
- Status: PRIMARY expository/review article; model-scope restriction explicit.

## [70] Tadeusz Bałaban, Michael O'Carroll, Ricardo Schor — Properties of block renormalization group operators for Euclidean Fermions in an external gauge field

- Full citation: T. Bałaban, M. O'Carroll, R. Schor, “Properties of block renormalization group operators for Euclidean Fermions in an external gauge field,” *Journal of Mathematical Physics* **32**(11), 3199–3208 (1991).
- DOI: 10.1063/1.529479
- Source used: https://www.researchwithrutgers.org/en/publications/properties-of-block-renormalization-group-operators-for-euclidean/
- Role in traversal: control evidence that Bałaban-style RG operator estimates are formulated as uniform analyticity in complex-valued gauge fields together with exponentially decaying kernels. Used only to motivate the complex-analytic compatibility obligation for the proposed soft gauge kernel; it is not a theorem about the 4d pure Yang–Mills soft-kernel modification.
- Status: PRIMARY peer-reviewed article; model-scope restriction explicit.
