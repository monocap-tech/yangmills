## [76] Michele Benzi and Nader Razouk — Decay bounds and O(n) algorithms for approximating functions of sparse matrices

- Full citation: M. Benzi and N. Razouk, “Decay bounds and O(n) algorithms for approximating functions of sparse matrices,” *Electronic Transactions on Numerical Analysis* **28**, 16–39 (2007/2008).
- Source used: https://etna.ricam.oeaw.ac.at/volumes/2001-2010/vol28/abstract.php?pages=16-39
- Role in traversal: YM-R30 control theorem for exponential off-diagonal decay of analytic matrix functions `f(A)` of sparse/banded operators. Used only as an abstract functional-calculus bridge showing that, under uniform finite-range/coercivity hypotheses, the covariance root `A^{-1/2}` inherits exponential decay. It is not a Yang–Mills or Bałaban-source theorem and does not supply external-background locality by itself.
- Status: PRIMARY peer-reviewed article; model-independent control source.

## [77] Tadeusz Bałaban — Regularity and decay of lattice Green's functions

- Full citation: T. Bałaban, “Regularity and decay of lattice Green's functions,” *Communications in Mathematical Physics* **89**(4), 571–597 (1983).
- DOI: 10.1007/BF01214744
- Source used: https://www.researchwithrutgers.org/en/publications/regularity-and-decay-of-lattice-greens-functions/
- Role in traversal: YM-R30 primary Bałaban source for positivity and exponential decay of Green functions of lattice covariant Laplace operators with external gauge fields, with bounds uniform in lattice spacing. This is upstream evidence for the native propagator/locality package later extended in CMP95/96 and consumed in CMP116.
- Status: PRIMARY peer-reviewed article.
