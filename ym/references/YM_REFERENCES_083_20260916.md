## [83] J. Dimock — The Renormalization Group According to Balaban III. Convergence

- Full citation: J. Dimock, “The Renormalization Group According to Balaban III. Convergence,” *Annales Henri Poincaré* **15** (2014), 2133–2175.
- DOI: 10.1007/s00023-013-0303-3.
- arXiv: 1304.0705.
- Source used: https://arxiv.org/abs/1304.0705
- Role in traversal: terminology audit for YM-R63. Despite the title “Convergence,” the paper treats the ultraviolet problem for the scalar phi^4 model in d=3, demonstrates convergence of the large-field/cluster expansion, and completes a cutoff-uniform stability bound for the partition function. It is therefore a control example for distinguishing convergence of an RG expansion from cross-cutoff convergence of normalized states or effective densities.
- Relevant source boundary: the paper’s stated goal is stability of Z_{M,N} uniformly in the ultraviolet cutoff N after counterterm tuning; its final corollary is a partition-function stability bound. It does not constitute the Yang–Mills stable-fiber/two-trajectory theorem isolated in YM-R62.
- Status: PRIMARY peer-reviewed article / arXiv full text.
