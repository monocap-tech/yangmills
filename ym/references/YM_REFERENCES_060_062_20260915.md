## [60] Paul Federbush — A phase cell approach to Yang-Mills theory VI: Non-abelian lattice-continuum duality

- Full citation: P. Federbush, “A phase cell approach to Yang-Mills theory. VI. Non-abelian lattice-continuum duality,” *Annales de l'Institut Henri Poincaré, Section A* **47**(1), 17–23 (1987).
- Source: https://www.numdam.org/item/AIHPA_1987__47_1_17_0.pdf
- Role in traversal: YM-R17 evidence for a block-spin map that agrees with a Balaban-type invariant-distance averaging prescription in the small-field regime and is given a suitable continuous extension to large-field regions. The paper explicitly cautions that its small-field definition differs slightly from Bałaban's original one.
- Status: PRIMARY.

## [61] F. Javier Trigos-Arrieta and Marian Turzański — On Aumann's theorem that the circle does not admit a mean

- Full citation: F. J. Trigos-Arrieta and M. Turzański, “On Aumann's theorem that the circle does not admit a mean,” *Acta Universitatis Carolinae. Mathematica et Physica* **46**(2) (2005), 77–82.
- Source used: https://www.impan.pl / DML mirror surfaced at https://www.researchgate.net/publication/2117297_On_Aumann%27s_Theorem_that_the_sphere_does_not_admit_a_mean and https://dml.cz/bitstream/handle/10338.dmlcz/702111/ActaCarolinae_046-2005-2_13.pdf
- Role in traversal: contextual topological confirmation that globally continuous symmetric idempotent means are obstructed even on S^1. The project also records an independent pi_1 derivation for the maximal-torus restriction relevant to a compact gauge group.
- Status: PRIMARY/ACADEMIC ARTICLE; use DML copy for formal citation.

## [62] Tadeusz Bałaban — Spaces of regular gauge field configurations on a lattice and gauge fixing conditions

- Full citation: T. Bałaban, “Spaces of regular gauge field configurations on a lattice and gauge fixing conditions,” *Communications in Mathematical Physics* **99**(1), 75–102 (1985).
- DOI: 10.1007/BF01466594
- Source: https://www.researchwithrutgers.org/en/publications/spaces-of-regular-gauge-field-configurations-on-a-lattice-and-gau/
- Role in traversal: confirms that Bałaban's analytic gauge machinery is organized on gauge-invariant spaces of regular configurations and suitable gauge-fixing surfaces, rather than furnishing a blanket analyticity statement over all lattice configurations.
- Status: PRIMARY publication metadata / peer-reviewed article.
