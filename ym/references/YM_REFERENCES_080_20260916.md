## Custody note

Consumed in YM-R51 because the primary author-uploaded full text was inspected directly for the general polymer Mayer-convergence theorem (Theorem 2) and its tree/leaf-stripping proof used by Bałaban CMP116 as reference [26] behind Eq. (2.29)-type summability. This is the first genuinely new scholarly source consumed after [79].

Relevant primary-source features used in R51:

- Theorem 2 is the general polymer Mayer-series convergence theorem; it is distinct from Theorem 1 on correlation decay.
- The hypothesis is organized by polymer cardinality through a rooted activity bound of the form used in (1.18).
- The proof rewrites connected-graph sums as tree sums and recursively sums from the leaves.
- The proof exposes the scalar convergence condition `epsilon/(1-epsilon) < 1/(e-1)`, hence any `epsilon < 1/e` is sufficient for that scalar step.

No stronger claim about an exact verbatim identification of Cammarota polymers with Bałaban localization domains is made; R51 supplies that adaptation as a separate derivation from CMP116’s finite-degree localization geometry and Eq. (2.30) metric lower bound.
