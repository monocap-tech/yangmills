## [59] Tadeusz Bałaban — Convergent renormalization expansions for lattice gauge theories

- Full citation: T. Bałaban, “Convergent renormalization expansions for lattice gauge theories,” *Communications in Mathematical Physics* **119**(2), 243–285 (1988).
- DOI: 10.1007/BF01217741
- Source used: https://www.researchwithrutgers.org/en/publications/convergent-renormalization-expansions-for-lattice-gauge-theories/
- Role in traversal: YM-R16 exactness audit. The paper introduces an inductive description of the **complete effective densities including large-field domains** and states that the renormalization transformations preserve the form of those densities. This prevents the earlier 4d small-field approximation from being mistaken for the final complete RG density.
- Status: PRIMARY publication metadata / peer-reviewed article.
