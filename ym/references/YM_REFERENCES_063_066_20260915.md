## [63] Palle Jorgensen and Feng Tian — Reflection positivity and spectral theory

- Citation: P. E. T. Jorgensen and F. Tian, “Reflection positivity and spectral theory,” arXiv:1705.05262 (2017).
- Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.05262
- Role in traversal: YM-R18 abstract context for the relation between Markov properties and Osterwalder–Schrader positivity. The paper states that the Markov property implies OS positivity and studies canonical factorization associated with RP systems.
- Status: PRIMARY PREPRINT / mathematical source.

## [64] Tadeusz Bałaban, Joel Feldman, Horst Knörrer, Eugene Trubowitz et al. — The Algebra of Block Spin Renormalization Group Transformations

- Citation source: arXiv:1609.00966 (2016).
- Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/1609.00966
- Role in traversal: rigorous constructive precedent for implementing an RG step by insertion of a normalized Gaussian block-spin integral `exp[-b <theta-Q psi, theta-Q psi>]`, and for exact composition/algebra of block-spin transformations.
- Status: PRIMARY PREPRINT; not a Yang–Mills theorem.

## [65] Tadeusz Bałaban, Michael O'Carroll, Ricardo Schor — Block averaging renormalization group for lattice and continuum Euclidean fermions: Expected and unexpected results

- Full citation: T. Bałaban, M. O'Carroll, R. Schor, “Block averaging renormalization group for lattice and continuum euclidean fermions: Expected and unexpected results,” *Letters in Mathematical Physics* **17**(3), 209–214 (1989).
- DOI: 10.1007/BF00401587
- Source: https://www.researchwithrutgers.org/en/publications/block-averaging-renormalization-group-for-lattice-and-continuum-e/
- Role in traversal: exact comparison of exponential-weight and delta-function block averaging. The abstract records scale-uniform exponential decay for the exponential-weight free-field RGT and failure of uniform exponential decay for the corresponding delta-function RGT.
- Status: PRIMARY.

## [66] K. Holland, A. Ipp, D. Müller, U. Wenger — Machine learning a fixed point action for SU(3) gauge theory with a gauge equivariant convolutional neural network

- Full citation: K. Holland, A. Ipp, D. Müller, U. Wenger, “Machine learning a fixed point action for SU(3) gauge theory with a gauge equivariant convolutional neural network,” *Physical Review D* **110**, 074502 (2024).
- DOI: 10.1103/PhysRevD.110.074502
- Source: https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.110.074502
- Open PDF used: https://scoap3-prod-backend.s3.cern.ch/media/files/88804/10.1103/PhysRevD.110.074502.pdf
- Role in traversal: concrete lattice-gauge soft blocking kernel `T[U,V] = -kappa sum ReTr(V Q^dagger) + normalization`, exact partition-function normalization, weak-coupling saddle-point limit, and an explicit blocked matrix `Q` built from smeared fine-link paths which need not itself lie in SU(N). Used as a candidate architecture/control example, not as a constructive Yang–Mills mass-gap theorem or an RP theorem.
- Status: PRIMARY peer-reviewed article.
