# YM-R76 — additive terminology registry

Date: 2026-10-10
Registry scope: R76 new load-bearing terms. Historical pass terminology unchanged.

- ReportedSchurScalarPivot: real finite-dimensional claim [[1,x^T],[x,X]]>=0 => X-xx^T>=0. Mathematical algebra certified here; exact P2M Lean provenance not verified.
- NativeSchurCarrier: a concrete pair of retained and eliminated fluctuation coordinates with native gauge/block constraints, Hessian or covariance choice, measure and normalization explicitly specified. NOT SOURCE-IDENTIFIED here.
- GaugeFixedEliminationBlock: the C block restricted to actual homogeneous constrained fluctuation slice; C>0 with a uniform lower bound is an additional hypothesis, not a PSD consequence.
- ReducedHessian: S=A-BC^(-1)B* for positive invertible eliminated C on a source-identified finite carrier. Differs from covariance conditional Schur and from the full integrated effective action.
- GaussianDeterminantContribution: (1/2)log det C to effective action from Gaussian integration, dependent on background even when S is fixed.
- PolarizationJetShiftDefect: compatible normalized C^2 momentum-jet norm of the difference between physically aligned full one-step vacuum-polarization tensors. Neither a scalar PSD nor an operator norm difference.
- SchurLocalityBridge: quantitative inverse/locality/derivative estimates for C^(-1), off-diagonal blocks, projectors and higher vertices sufficient to control a remainder norm; OPEN and not a claim of existence.
- PrimaryOneStepVacuumPolarizationStationarity: R75 native gate, unchanged and OPEN.
- PrimaryEq029PositiveAlphaBound: R69-R75 native gate, unchanged and OPEN.

Classification terms: CERTIFIED means a proven mathematical implication within its stated assumptions; only source-native applications may count as closure of a Yang-Mills gate. CONDITIONAL means hypotheses are unverified in native sources; OBSTRUCTED means a proposed implication is false as stated; OPEN means insufficient evidence.
