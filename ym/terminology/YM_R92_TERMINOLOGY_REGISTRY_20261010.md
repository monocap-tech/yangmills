# YM-R92 — additive terminology registry

Date: 2026-10-10. Prior head YM-R91 / edfc40c6339e152866eb69250d9ae83eb3f5bd7a.
Append-only; historical definitions unchanged.

- OneMarkedRootedPolymerResponse: response of an abstract hard-core polymer logarithm to variation of one ordinary polymer weight, keeping all others bounded by one common majorant.
- RootedClusterSusceptibility: T_(nu,p)(Y)=sum_(n>=1) 1/(n-1)! sum_(Y2,...,Yn)|phi_T(Y,Y2,...,Yn)| product_(j>=2)(p_(Yj)e^(nu|Yj|)); assumes T<=exp(a|Y|) for all Y under a *strong* uniform weighted KP condition.
- MarkedReplacementKernel: r_x(Y)=partial_(t_x)w_t(Y) with uniform bound |r_x(Y)|<=u_x exp(-lambda_m |Y|-sigma dist(x,Y)) on all partial insertion environments; unverified for CMP116.
- MarkedRootToRootTransfer: if z lies in Y, dist(x,z)<=dist(x,Y)+|Y|-1, so a positive decay theta from x to z may be retained after spending theta in the activity-size exponent.
- MarkedAnimalThreshold: t=exp[-(lambda_m-a-nu-theta)], q=C_ent*t<1; controls sum of rooted mark weights by u_x exp(-theta dist(x,z)) t/(1-q).
- GhostPolymerSeed: auxiliary one-use polymer g_x with weight v_x and declared incompatibility with ordinary polymers touching its physical seed; represents an independently added seed even when native D is empty, but is not a source-identified CMP116 object.
- GhostPartitionIdentity: Xi_aug=Xi+w_g Xi_compatible(g), for finite hard-core gas with one optional ghost polymer; exact before logarithms.
- GhostRatioBound: if common analytic connected-cluster estimates bound |log(Xi_compatible/Xi)|<=A_g, then |w_g| e^(A_g)<1 yields |log(Xi_aug/Xi)|<=-log(1-|w_g|e^(A_g)).
- ConditionalMarkedDClosure: algebraic one-marked replacement/ghost estimates closed under stated rooted KP/seed assumptions; native CMP116 one-marked D/P/Z0/Z0' source term still OPEN.
- CammarotaDStageVersusKPLog: CMP116 Eq (2.29) D-family positive sum (R51, Cammarota theorem 2) and later connected-log hard-core cluster summability (KP [87]) are different combinatorial operations. Neither theorem silently supplies CMP116 tagged bounds.
- NonemptyDFamilyQuantifier: D-stage bound <=1 applies to a constrained nonempty/fixed-Y0 sum; an unrestricted nonnegative sum INCLUDING empty D already contains 1 and cannot be <=1 when other positive terms exist.
- ActualExtraLayerCarrierAndAlpha: primary CMP109 Eq (0.29), first-layer V^(1) type, positive-alpha, source normalization, common coupling/gauge and full R/B boundary remain OPEN.

ABSTRACT-DERIVED means conditional algebraic mathematics, NOT a native four-dimensional Yang-Mills theorem or a mass-gap certificate.
