# YM-AUX-01 terminology (2026-10-28)

**Full-periodic mean identity.** The identity-background, unrescaled full CMP109 coarse-link derivative satisfies mean_y(Da)_mu = L mean_x(a_mu) when all centered blocks partition a complete periodic lattice and all endpoint paths and bridges are included.

**Constant harmonic subspace.** Constant Lie-algebra-valued positive-axis 1-forms on the finite periodic torus; dimension 12 for d=4 and SU(2).

**Zero-mode allocation.** The exact mean identity implies ker D is orthogonal to constant Lie-algebra 1-forms in the standard positive-bond metric. It does not eliminate all individual winding-loop variations.

**Regional boundary defect.** On a proper Ω/Λ with missing coarse origins or contours, endpoint sums need not telescope and bridge multiplicities need not be uniform.

**Coercivity non-transfer.** Elimination of zero-frequency modes from the fixed-average tangent does not imply a quantitative lower bound for the complete native reduced Gaussian precision K.

**Canonical jurisdiction.** YM-R150 remains unconsumed pending actual CMP99 precision images or a source-compatible finite K/domain.
