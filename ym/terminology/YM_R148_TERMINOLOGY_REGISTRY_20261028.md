- **Conditional monitored variance:** for identity SU(2) Gaussian Q=K⊗I3, Var(x_a | all other monitored bonds)=K_aa^-1 I3.
- **Marginal monitored variance:** (K^-1)_aa I3, generally different and used for Maxwell cutoff tails.
- **Gate A:** anti-concentration of two translated radial balls; consumes an UPPER conditional precision K_aa and marginal z variance.
- **Gate B:** rare-exit union of two balls; consumes UPPER marginal variances, which can follow from a source lower bound K≥α I. Choose this gate first when cutoff is far in the tail.
- **T_k:** monitored threshold after Gaussian scaling, θ_k/|g_k|; keep ε1 vs g_k ε1/γ_k distinct.
- **Same-weight normalized comparison:** cutoff regions A,B applied to ONE positive weight W and ONE reference Gaussian; exact TV depends on unmatched weighted masses and partition functions.
- **Cross-UV law:** different histories generally have different W, Gaussian precision, gauge, Ω, inherited E and normalizers. Same-weight result does not close it.
