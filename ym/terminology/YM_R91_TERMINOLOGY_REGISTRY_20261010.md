# YM-R91 — additive terminology registry

Date: 2026-10-10. Prior completed head: YM-R90 / 465d8290647a8c8f2647d76b985279731f0275ca.
Append-only terminology; older meanings preserved.

- MarkedFactorReplacement: differentiation substitutes one tag weight u_x in place of one original small D-activity weight epsilon1; unlike a multiplicative insertion it need not carry epsilon1*u_x.
- MarkedSeedAdjoining: a tag creates a new required source-owned connected seed, possibly in a summand with D empty. Needs a marked index family and Z0/Y0 hull dictionary; cannot be reconstructed by differentiating only existing D-factors.
- ScalarMarkedGeometricSeries: f(epsilon)=sum_(n>=1) K^n epsilon^n, with tagged factor replacement bound u sum_(n>=1)n K^n epsilon^(n-1)=K*u/(1-K epsilon)^2 when K epsilon<1. Illustrative generic majorant, NOT a claim that CMP116 families equal the geometric series.
- MarkedPositiveResummationGate: a bound on the sum of positive tagged termwise majorants, with one u_x factor and attachment distance, before concluding a tagged H(Z) bound. Original unmarked endpoint alone is insufficient.
- MandatoryMarkConnectivity: every term contributing to a tagged output carries a source-owned path or seed connecting the mark to the output localization set; this must be proved with actual CMP116 weakening/root support.
- SingleInputComplexRayAlternative: holomorphic dependence on ONE inserted amplitude t_x with an admissible radius proportional to 1/u_x, uniform over all other partial insertion environments; may recover O(u_x) by Cauchy without a global Banach input ball. Requires source analytic admissibility in insertion amplitudes, not merely background U^c analyticity.
- MarkedOperatorMotion: variation of conditioning, covariance, gauge chart, cutoff, or localization operator. Exact derivative includes (partial_t L_t)[G_t] in addition to L_t[partial_t G_t].
- NormalizedGaussianCovarianceVariation: for finite SPD C_t and regular F_t, d_t E_(C_t)[F_t]=E_(C_t)[d_t F_t]+(1/2)sum_ab (d_t C_t)_ab E_(C_t)[partial_a partial_b F_t].
- UniformMarkedDStageReserve: the source D-family/tree summability must have spare decay/smallness after replacing one epsilon1-weight; ordinary convergence of the unmarked sum does not establish the exact tagged constant.
- PrimaryEq029Gate: uninspected original CMP109 Eq(0.29), residual V^(1), positive-alpha and first-common-scale comparison, retained OPEN.
- MultiSectorCrossCutoffDebt: coupling/marginal, R/B large-field, gauge and measure sectors and beta/F2 normalization not discharged by a regular tagged lemma.

ABSTRACT-DERIVED statements have complete algebraic proofs but are not native Yang-Mills certifications. Source audit R50/R51/R53 remains prior-custodied unmarked evidence.
