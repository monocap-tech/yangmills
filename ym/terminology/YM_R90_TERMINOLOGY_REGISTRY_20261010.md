# YM-R90 — additive terminology registry

Date: 2026-10-10
Predecessor: YM-R89 checkpoint dafca6bb7db364e5086a3851a485625f681835bc.
Historical definitions are retained unchanged.

- FrozenNativeLocalizationOperator: the CMP116 conditioning, product-Gaussian, weakening and D/P/Z0/Z0' localization machinery represented on a fixed source index family with all covariance, cutoff, carrier, gauge and chart data held fixed as tagged input amplitudes vary. This is an application *hypothesis*, not a claim about the actual two-cutoff comparison.
- MovingLocalizationOperator: localization/conditioning/coordinate data depend on the interpolation parameters; their derivatives generate additional operator, covariance, Jacobian, cutoff and seed-index contributions.
- TaggedTermwiseDomination: a uniform estimate |partial_(t_x)T_i(t)|<=C_ins u_x (1+d(Z))^p exp[-sigma dist(pi(x),Z)] M_i for every original term i contributing to H_Z and all partial environments; M_i are nonnegative absolute majorants.
- PositiveResummationEnvelope: sum_(i in I_Z) M_i<=h exp[-lambda_H d(Z)] obtained by an *absolute* majorant, not from |H_Z|<=h exp[-lambda_H d(Z)] alone.
- MarkedReplacementAmplitude: a tagged insertion may replace rather than multiply one original epsilon1 activity factor. In that case it requires its own positive tagged majorant with prefactor C_tag u_x, not an unjustified h*u_x gain.
- PolynomialMarkMultiplicity: (1+d(Z))^p from differentiation of finitely many source factors or placements, to be absorbed by spending theta>0 of the exponential reserve; an estimate, not a native assertion.
- ReservedTaggedDecay: lambda_tag=lambda_H-theta>0, with theta chosen strictly below the source exponentiation reserve where simultaneous later uses demand it; no double spending.
- TaggedTreeMetricDictionary: a source-verified comparison between CMP116 d_(k+1)(Z) and minimal cube-tree attachment tau(Z,pi(x)). Not closed by notation alone.
- FixedCarrierDifferentiationGate: uniform absolute convergence and unchanged index/seed family that permits partial_(t_x) sum_i T_i=sum_i partial_(t_x)T_i.
- TaggedSourceOperatorDerivativeGate: for moving localization, prove/estimate (partial_(t_x)L_t)[G_t] in addition to L_t[partial_(t_x)G_t].
- FirstLayerSourceInsertionDictionary: original CMP109 Eq(0.29), correct marginal-subtracted V^(1) and comparison with the actual CMP116 potential; still OPEN.
- NativeTaggedR90ClosureGate: one source-identified tagged termwise majorant plus preserved absolute D/P/Z0/Z0' resummation in all partial environments, with actual gauge/measure and beta-sector matching; remains OPEN.
