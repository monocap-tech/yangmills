# YM-R146 — Terminology registry

- **Constraint transport S:** a map between *different* old/new averaging-constraint kernels D_o,D_n when a common chosen right inverse exists (R142/R143). This is not the same as pivot exchange.
- **Pivot exchange T:** chart change on the SAME `ker D` from deleting bond a to deleting bond b; determinant −κ_b/κ_a per real color (R146).
- **Physical χ:** source's indicator on declared pre-linearization monitored bonds, **not** a coordinate box redefined after changing pivot.
- **Pulled-back χ:** exact same physical event expressed as χ(C_a x), generally an oblique polytope.
- **Gaussian chart invariance:** a change-of-variables identity with precision congruence and its determinant/Jacobian; does not imply the two different RG source laws coincide.
- **Flat coordinate norm versus volume:** |det T|=1 does not imply ||T||=1 or a uniformly controlled operator family.
