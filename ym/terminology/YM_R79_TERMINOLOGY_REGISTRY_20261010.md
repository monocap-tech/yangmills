Historical terminology unchanged. Definitions below are entered before/with first load-bearing use.

- LocalBackgroundInsertion: a source derivative V_x=partial_(b_x) C(b)|_0 of a *finite* positive gauge-fixed precision operator, supported in a uniformly bounded neighborhood P_x of root x; not yet identified with an actual CMP109 fluctuation derivative.
- DoubleInsertionContact: W_xy=partial_(b_x)partial_(b_y) C(b)|_0, supported on uniformly bounded-rank coordinates and vanishing beyond a fixed separation R_w, as an explicit extra hypothesis.
- GaussianPolarizationKernel: Pi^G_xy = (1/2)Tr(X W_xy - X V_x X V_y), X=C(0)^-1, the mixed background derivative of (1/2)logdet C in a fixed finite Lebesgue integration chart. Not automatically the full source vacuum-polarization tensor.
- UniformInsertionRank: r bounding rank(P_x), and r_w bounding rank of support of W_xy, independent of volume/cutoff/position.
- LocalPropagatorDecay: ||P_x X P_y|| <= G exp(-kappa(d(x,y)-2R)_+), where P_x and P_y are local supports of radius at most R.
- AlignedLocalPropagatorDefect: the same projected decay inequality for X_1-X_0, with prefactor delta_G, on *identified* physically aligned finite carriers. Distinct Hilbert dimensions require a separately proved embedding/determinant normalization dictionary.
- LocalTracePerRootBound: pointwise bound on Pi^G_xy from local insertion rank and propagator decay, independent of total mode count; not a bound for (1/2)logdet C itself or all polarization components.
- FullPolarizationRemainder: source-identified residual Pi - Pi^G including all remaining measure, non-Gaussian vertex/cumulant, gauge, and localization contributions. This is a formal audit bucket, not an asserted native Bałaban decomposition.
- SourceNativeTraceInsertionDictionary: original-primary identity linking any Bałaban local polarization component to LocalBackgroundInsertion/DoubleInsertionContact, including the gauge chart, scalar momentum extraction and all normalizations; OPEN.
- PrimaryCMP109EquationWindow: original Eqs (5.37), (5.42), (0.29) and adjacent definitions, hypotheses and quantitative constants. Still UNINSPECTED.

Classification: ABSTRACT-DERIVED = elementary finite-dimensional theorem within exact hypotheses; CONDITIONAL-NATIVE = correct *if* original Yang–Mills objects and hypotheses are proved; OPEN-PRIMARY = equation text not inspected; OBSTRUCTED = the generic implication is false. No continuum Yang–Mills or mass-gap claim follows.
