# YM-AUX-05 terminology and obligation types

- **Source bond average Q1:** historical CMP99 bond-field linearized averaging used in the constraint Q1 B=0; independent special bonds reconstructed through C. NOT site-gauge Q'.
- **Source site-gauge average Q':** operates on site gauge transformations and can be defined by block-centered parallel-transported gauge averages; its kernel determines the projection R on a site Hilbert carrier.
- **Projected Landau:** R D* A=0, equivalent in finite dimensions to D*A in ran(Q'^*), not ordinary co-closedness. Source A is the transformed field, not necessarily original Gaussian B.
- **Ordinary co-closed:** D*a=0, a stronger abstract AUX-04 hypothesis; source gauge matching OPEN.
- **Flux increment functional:** Phi(s)-Phi(s-1)=-<Q'1_s,lambda> if projected gauge gives D*a=Q'^*lambda, in an explicitly matching discrete metric and domain.
- **Zero extension alone:** zero outside proper region or on one complete normal slice; without co-closedness or equivalent flux cancellation, does not force all flux to vanish.
- **One-step countermodel:** a=partial-sum primitive of blockwise site Q'^*lambda on periodic N=169, L=13, with support 7..31. No satisfaction of source bond Q1B=0 or full CMP99 multiscale gauge presumed.
- **Native gating:** actual CMP99/CMP109 Q1, Q', R, C, gauge A/B transformation, source Ω/Λ and K still unverified. YM-R150 UNCONSUMED on SOURCE_DATA_HOLD.
