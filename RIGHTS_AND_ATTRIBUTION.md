# Rights, reuse, and attribution

**Prepared:** 2026-10-26. **Owner decision pending:** a repository-wide license has **not** been selected or added.

The repository is public for reading and research inspection. **Public visibility does not itself grant blanket permission to reproduce, redistribute, or relicence its contents** outside rights that GitHub's terms or applicable law provide. Do not advertise the project as “open source” until a suitable license has been deliberately selected by the rights holder.

The repository includes independently authored or AI-assisted analysis, bibliographic descriptions, source locators, links to third-party work, and possibly formula transcriptions. **A license chosen for original repository material would not cover separately copyrighted publisher articles, external manuscript drafts, or other material whose rights are held by third parties.**

## Attribution and research credit

- Cite the exact source paper and equation when a mathematical statement derives from Bałaban or subsequent authors.
- Distinguish internally checked algebra, an AI-generated proposal, an external manuscript claim, and an independently verified theorem.
